=== KOF STYLE SYSTEMS ===


You are given two different modes to choose from at the start of a match.
These modes are STANDARD and ADVANCED. These two modes hold very different mechanics entirely,
and each character using this style capitalizes off of these systems in very different ways.


====================
===== STANDARD =====
====================

Description: Standard Mode is good for people learning this new style. Essentially, it has pretty
simple tools and mechanics that aren't too heavy in execution to master.

==========
Mechanics
==========

Dash -- Double tap forward and you do a quick hop step.

Roll -- Hold Forward then Press A and C and you do a roll that evades all attacks, 
however you can be thrown out of this roll. You can also hold backward instead of forward
to do a backwards roll instead.

Crushing Blow -- Press both B and D and you do a powerful attack that can link
into other attacks, however if blocked, this move is easily punishable. It is completely
invulnerable on startup, making it a useful wake-up tool.

Enhanced Specials -- Press 2 attack buttons instead of 1 when performing a special
to have an Enhanced Special. This move is generally more powerful than the regular
specials and has different properties. This move costs half a bar of meter.

Power Charge -- Hold A+B+C and you slowly begin to charge your power. It goes slow
in the beginning, but gets faster as you charge for longer.

Super Jump -- Press down and then up, and you perform a jump with an incredible vertical distance.
Hold back or forward and you travel at a decent horizontal velocity.

MAX Super at 3 bars -- When you do a super with 2 attack buttons at full meter, you
perform a MAX super, which is a much more powerful version of the super you inputted for.

Just Defend -- Guard attack at just the right time and you get Just Defend. Just Defend gives you both life and
meter, and acts just like the Just Defend from games like CvS2 and Garou Mark of the Wolves.

Tech Roll -- Press A and B when you are close to the ground and you perform a Tech Roll,
which helps you recover much quicker than before during a knockdown.

Guard Counter -- Press both C and D when blocking and you perform a counter attack that will
hit the opponent. Since it's a counter, it's much more powerful than normal. It costs half
a bar of meter.


====================
===== ADVANCED =====
====================

Description: Advanced mode is for people who have learned this character to a fair degree, and this
mode awards their skill with buffs that capitalize on very key aspects of the fight. Instead of 3 bars,
Advanced mode simplifies it to 1 bar, so meter gaining is heavily increased.

==========
Mechanics
==========

Run -- Double tap and then hold forward to run. Let go of forward to stop running.

Dodge -- Press A and C and you will perform a quick dodge. You cannot be hit during this dodge, but you
can be thrown out of it. Press any attack button during the dodge, and you perform a Blowaway attack.

Increased Juggling -- Advanced Mode's main feature is the fact that it has advanced combos that can
deal a lot of damage in return for heavy execution. The juggle points are decreased, which allows for
much more combo creativity and variety.

Super Cancelling -- When performing a special, you can quickly perform a super command once the special
hits, and you will cancel into the super. This is a main part of Advanced mode's increased combo potential.

EX Mode -- When you have more than a full bar of eter, press A+B+C and you enter EX Mode. You get a
25% attack boost along with more juggling opportunities, and during MAX mode, if you reach full meter
again, you can perform the MAX super, however if you're lower than 25% health you still need to reach
full meter again to perform the MAX Super.

MAX Super at low health -- If you're lower than 25% health and you reach full meter, your super becomes
a MAX Super, but you still lose the meter.

Hyper Hop -- When running, press up during the run and you perform a very fast but not vertical jump. This is
great for closing in space and starting rushdown, and is a great start to combos.

Just Defend -- Guard attack at just the right time and you get Just Defend. Just Defend gives you both life and
meter, and acts just like the Just Defend from games like CvS2 and Garou Mark of the Wolves.

Stationary Tech -- Press A and B when you are close to the ground and you perform a Stationary Tech.
You'll recover much quicker, but you'll stand still.

Counter Movement -- Press both C and D when blocking and you perform a roll. By holding forward or backwards,
you can change which direction you roll. If you don't input anything, then it defaults to a forwards roll.
This move costs half of the meter.
