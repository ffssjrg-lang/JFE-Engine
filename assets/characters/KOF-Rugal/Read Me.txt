- KOF-Rugal Readme -


  = Q & A =


 Q : Why Rugal? In a KOF Style instead of the CvS one you usually do?

 A : I felt like I wanted to expand my coding talent farther than just CvS. I decided to not only do a KOF
     style, but I decided to make Rugal as my first KOF character since he's the one I know how to play most.


 Q : What's his playstyle like?

 A : Rugal plays like his KOF '98 appearance, along with moves taken from his Omega Rugal mode.


 Q : How do you play Ukyo?

 A : Rugal is very slow, but makes up for it in high damage combos. His Advanced mode really brings out the combo
     ability in his moves, and Rugal becomes an offensive monster. Rugal's Fwd+C is the main method to starting out
     a majority of his BnB combos, and his special moves take care of the rest of the combo. His A Dark Smash can be
     comboed into from aerial D, so his rushdown is also incredibly good with Hyper Hop. It's important to play a
     slow and steady neutral game while also knowing the many combos Rugal has access to when you land an attack.
